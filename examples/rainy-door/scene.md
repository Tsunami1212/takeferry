# Rain at the door

**INT. APARTMENT LANDING, NIGHT**

Rain ticks against a steel window. An walks past the door with a letter folded into her hand. A key turns behind her. Only then does she look back.

## The writer's note

Use the portrait for An's face and the doorway for light. Keep the rain. I want five seconds in portrait format. She turns at two seconds. Give me one locked shot first.

After seeing it, try the same shot on the other platform. Keep the lighting and timing. A second camera choice can creep forward. If I ask for a slower push, branch from the moving take.

## Sample assets

The two PNGs and three five-second MP4s in `takeferry/data/rainy-door/` are original procedural storyboards. The key sound begins at two seconds. The fixed and moving versions share the same character, lighting and timing. The offline example uses these files as its returned media. The drawing and audio source is [make_storyboards.py](make_storyboards.py).
